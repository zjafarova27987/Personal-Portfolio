Logic Gates Project

The Logic Gates project was one of the four projects in the SITE 1101 course. My team and I were supposed to build three logic gates — OR, AND, and NOT — along with one additional bonus gate, XOR. The main idea was to film the entire process, explain how the gates work, and practically demonstrate how to build them. As a team, we worked really well together, and every team member was involved in the project. My main role was video editing. This project helped us develop our teamwork skills and learn many things about logic gates. We gained a better understanding of their concepts and how they work.
